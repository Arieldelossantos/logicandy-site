Help and contact

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Here are quick answers, and how to reach us.

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone. Deleting the app deletes it, but your phone’s own backup (Android backup or iPhone backup) can bring it back when you restore or reinstall.

How does the daily reminder work?

Turn it on in Settings, Daily reminder, and pick a time. It only comes on days you haven’t solved a daily yet. Turn it off in the same place.

Settings says “Blocked in the phone’s settings”.

Your phone isn’t letting Logicandy send notifications. Tap Open settings next to it, allow notifications, and come back.

A puzzle looks impossible.

Every board has exactly one solution you can reach by logic, with no guessing. A hint shows the next move and explains why.

How do I change the difficulty?

In Settings, Difficulty, each game has its own. Hardcore asks for a perfect solve.

Contact

Write to us at logicandy.help@gmail.com. We read every message.
