Logicandy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Logicandy keeps your progress on your phone. Ads and purchases go through Google, Apple and RevenueCat, as explained below.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your progress: solved levels, stars, crowns, medals, your daily results and streak, the critters you have collected, and your hint tokens.

Your settings: difficulty, theme, language, sound, vibration and the daily reminder.

All of it stays on your phone. If you use your phone’s own backup (Google or Apple), it is part of that backup, which we never see.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone at the time you pick. Nothing is sent to a server.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes a picture and a short text of your daily results and hands them to your phone’s share sheet. You choose where they go; the app sends nothing by itself.

What the app does not do

No account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.

Apart from ads, purchases and crash reports (below), the app makes no network requests of its own.

Ads

Logicandy shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad between some levels, and an ad you can choose to watch for a hint token. There are never ads during a puzzle or after a daily challenge. Remove ads turns off the ads between levels; the ads you choose to watch stay available.

To show and measure ads, Google’s ads software collects data such as your device’s advertising ID, an approximate location from your internet address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and the UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, About, Privacy choices.

On iPhone, the app does not ask to track you, so ads don’t use your advertising identifier.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Remove ads and hint packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

RevenueCat keeps an anonymous record of your purchases, so Restore purchases can bring Remove ads back on a new phone. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Crash reports

When something goes wrong in the app, it sends an error report to Sentry, the service we use to find and fix bugs: what went wrong and where in the app’s code, the app version, your phone model and system version, and the last screens you opened. Reports contain nothing that identifies you, and Sentry doesn’t keep your internet address. The app also tells Sentry, with no other detail, each time it’s opened, so we can see how often each version crashes.

You can turn them off at any time in Settings, About, Send crash reports.

Sentry’s privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Ratings

If you rate Logicandy from the dialog your phone shows, the rating goes to Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Logicandy is meant for players aged 13 and up, and is not directed at children.

Changes

When the app changes what it does with data (for example, if ads or purchases are added), this page is updated first, with a new date.

Who we are and how to reach us

Logicandy is made by AralaDls. For anything about privacy, write to logicandy.help@gmail.com.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it kept on your phone. If you write to us, we use your address and your message only to answer you, and delete them once they are no longer needed.

Questions? See Help and contact.
